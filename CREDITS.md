# Credits and asset rights

The Home routing approach was informed by [dumbtv](https://github.com/humblemuzzu/dumbtv). Its MIT notice is retained in `third-party/dumbtv-MIT.txt`.

Original YouTube, Prime Video, Netflix, Disney+, Google and PlayStation wordmarks identify their respective services. Logo paths were sourced from public brand/Wikimedia vector assets; dark-surface wordmarks use reversed lettering without changing their geometry. The source SVGs are retained in `logos/`. Google uses its original color wordmark. These trademarks are not covered by this repository's code license, and their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Poster artwork and screenshot footage are promotional/reference media belonging to the respective rights holders. They are excluded from the MIT license. Trailer MP4 files are not redistributed in Git. Review brand guidelines and media rights before reusing artwork in another product.

Optional runtime upstreams:
- [youtubedl-android 0.18.1](https://github.com/yausername/youtubedl-android): Python / FFmpeg runtime packaging; upstream GPL license notice included.
- [yt-dlp 2026.10.26](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/tag/2026.10.26): upstream license included.
- [yt-dlp EJS 0.8.0](https://github.com/yt-dlp/ejs): JavaScript challenge support.
- [Termux packages](https://github.com/termux/termux-packages): Node and runtime dependencies, versions and hashes in `third-party/native-preview-runtime/termux-node-packages.json`. Individual dependencies retain their respective upstream licenses.

Runtime binaries are downloaded locally, not committed or released here. A code license does not replace those upstream licenses.

The four streaming cards use TV banners from installed applications, retaining their original backgrounds and aspect ratios. Google uses its original color wordmark on white. PlayStation uses its original white symbol and wordmark over a launcher-drawn blue gradient; this background is an interpretation, not an official promotional card. Earlier promotional source URLs remain in `promotional-art.json`. These shortcuts do not install mobile Google or PlayStation apps.

The optional ambient footage is [Waterfall landscape by Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-video/waterfall-landscape-572/), under the [Mixkit Stock Video Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/). The standalone footage is not committed to this repository. Screenshots show it within the launcher interface.
